A simple file manager for organizing the files on your phone.

FEATURES
• Browse internal storage and SD cards
• Open with other apps, and share
• Rename, copy, move, delete and create folders
• Sort by name, date or size; show hidden files
• Install APK files

ABOUT "ALL FILES ACCESS"
The app uses this permission to show and organize the files on your device, as a file manager. It has no internet permission, so your files are never sent anywhere.

PRIVACY PROMISE
• No internet permission. The app has no way to send your data anywhere.
• No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no third-party trackers.
• No account needed. Everything stays on your device.
• No automatic cloud backup.

The Secured series is a set of small, lightweight apps that replace the everyday basics without doing anything extra: Camera, Gallery, Videos, Music, Notes, Calendar, Calculator, Alarm and Files.
