A simple month-view calendar.

FEATURES
• Month view, with a dot on days that have events
• Add, edit and delete events
• All-day events, repeating events (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly), location and notes
• Event reminders

EVENTS STAY ON YOUR DEVICE
New events are saved to an "On this device" calendar that is never synced anywhere. You can also see, and save to, calendars already set up on your phone. The app itself never goes online.

PRIVACY PROMISE
• No internet permission. The app has no way to send your data anywhere.
• No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no third-party trackers.
• No account needed. Everything stays on your device.
• No automatic cloud backup.

The Secured series is a set of small, lightweight apps that replace the everyday basics without doing anything extra: Camera, Gallery, Videos, Music, Notes, Calendar, Calculator, Alarm and Files.
