A simple notepad for writing things down.

FEATURES
• Saves automatically, with no save button
• The first line becomes the title in the list
• Search across all your notes
• Share a note
• Share text from other apps to create a new note

NO PERMISSIONS AT ALL
Notes are stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Note contents are hidden from the recent apps screen on Android 13 and later.

PRIVACY PROMISE
• No internet permission. The app has no way to send your data anywhere.
• No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no third-party trackers.
• No account needed. Everything stays on your device.
• No automatic cloud backup.

The Secured series is a set of small, lightweight apps that replace the everyday basics without doing anything extra: Camera, Gallery, Videos, Music, Notes, Calendar, Calculator, Alarm and Files.
