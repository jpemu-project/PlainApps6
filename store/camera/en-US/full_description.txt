A camera that does one thing: take photos and videos.

FEATURES
• Photos and videos, front and back cameras
• Flash (off / auto / on) and a light for video
• Pinch to zoom, tap to focus
• Volume buttons work as the shutter
• QR code scanner for links, Wi-Fi and text. Links are never opened automatically, so you can check the address first
• Works when other apps ask for a photo

NO LOCATION DATA
The app does not even have location permission, so your photos never contain where they were taken.

PRIVACY PROMISE
• No internet permission. The app has no way to send your data anywhere.
• No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no third-party trackers.
• No account needed. Everything stays on your device.
• No automatic cloud backup.

The Secured series is a set of small, lightweight apps that replace the everyday basics without doing anything extra: Camera, Gallery, Videos, Music, Notes, Calendar, Calculator, Alarm and Files.
