A gallery for browsing the photos and videos on your phone, album by album.

FEATURES
• Albums and an "All photos" view
• Pinch or double-tap to zoom, swipe to the next photo
• Video playback
• Trash, so deleted photos can be restored
• Photo details: date, size, resolution
• Opens photos from other apps

LOCATION REMOVED WHEN SHARING
When you share a JPEG photo, the app sends a copy with the GPS location and device serial numbers removed. Your original photo is not changed.

Supports Android 14's "selected photos only" access.

PRIVACY PROMISE
• No internet permission. The app has no way to send your data anywhere.
• No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no third-party trackers.
• No account needed. Everything stays on your device.
• No automatic cloud backup.

The Secured series is a set of small, lightweight apps that replace the everyday basics without doing anything extra: Camera, Gallery, Videos, Music, Notes, Calendar, Calculator, Alarm and Files.
