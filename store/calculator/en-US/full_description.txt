A simple calculator for everyday math.

FEATURES
• Add, subtract, multiply, divide, parentheses and percent
• Shows the result as you type
• Exact decimal math, so 0.1 + 0.2 is exactly 0.3
• Long-press ⌫ to clear everything
• Works in landscape

Uses no permissions at all.

PRIVACY PROMISE
• No internet permission. The app has no way to send your data anywhere.
• No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no third-party trackers.
• No account needed. Everything stays on your device.
• No automatic cloud backup.
